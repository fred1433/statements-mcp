- Salaries and wages
- Payroll taxes and benefits

Travel is probably inside "Other general and administrative", but the statements don't break that line down. I won't guess at a number. I also can't tell you whether travel sits in that line or in another one.

I can pull the Q2 quarter-to-date figure for "Other general and administrative", overall or by unit (Wholesale, Online, Service). That would be an upper bound on travel, not the travel amount. A travel figure would have to come from the general ledger or an expense report.
