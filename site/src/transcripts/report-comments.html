Claude's answer

Yes, but only in one place. The Q2 report has a single comment that touches on Wholesale. It says: "Wholesale: deliveries under the Harbor County schools supply agreement began in April." The comment is attributed to the Controller, dated July 8, 2026 ([IS_2026-06.xlsx]Total!A36).

Wholesale net sales were $3,915,153 in Q1 ([IS_2026-03.xlsx]Wholesale!C13) and $4,705,089 in Q2 ([IS_2026-06.xlsx]Wholesale!C13). That is an increase of +$789,936, or +20.2% (inputs: [IS_2026-03.xlsx]Wholesale!C13, [IS_2026-06.xlsx]Wholesale!C13).

The comment doesn't say the agreement caused the growth, and it gives no dollar amount for it. The statements hold amounts, not reasons. The comment gives a timing fact that fits the Q2 increase, but the report doesn't tie the two together or say how much of the +$789,936 came from that agreement. I also found no other commentary on Wholesale in the report.
